The axiom inspection has two separate outcomes.
\texttt{XiKernel.centerMoment\_pos} and the
small algebraic claims depend on the usual Mathlib axioms
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound}. The rational-jet computation instead
uses \texttt{native\_decide}, introducing
\texttt{Lean.ofReduceBool} and \texttt{Lean.trustCompiler}.
It is a Lean-compiled, compiler-assisted check of a
finite rational approximation, but does \emph{not} certify
the exact infinite theta series by Lean's small kernel.
The independently derived rational-interval Python
certificate proves a different claim: it encloses the
actual fifth iterate under the explicit analytic majorants
given in Section~1 and the companion source.

There are three priority proof obligations.
First, close the end-to-end Lean bridge from
the actual theta series to the fifth-iterate rational
interval, including analyticity, exact differentiation,
Machin and exponential estimates, tail control, and
sound outward arithmetic
(\href{https://github.com/tonynewton79-web/RiemannHypothesis/issues/1}{Issue 1}).
Second, certify noncentral transform-level
Laguerre inequalities over whole real intervals, and
seek genuinely all-order information rather than
pointwise high-precision evidence
(\href{https://github.com/tonynewton79-web/RiemannHypothesis/issues/2}{Issue 2}).
Third, independently audit the newly established
radially increasing curvature theorem
$(-\log\Phi)'''(u)>0$ on every $u>0$
(\href{https://github.com/tonynewton79-web/RiemannHypothesis/issues/3}{Issue 3});
its 500-cell interval certificate is exact Python arithmetic,
not an end-to-end Lean theorem.

\paragraph{Scope and prior art.}
The all-order correlation identity is closely related to
the Fourier-transform Wronskian and correlation
characterizations of Dimitrov--Xu~\cite{DX}; the
second-level concavity of the true theta kernel
is already established by Planat--Sol\'e~\cite{PS}.
Strict infinite log-concavity of a \emph{different}
Taylor-coefficient sequence was proved by
Liu--Mao~\cite{LM}. Neither those results nor our local
Laguerre band constitutes a proof of RH.
The fifth-iterate obstruction and all-iterate
Gaussian counterexamples warrant further specialist
review before originality or publication claims.
