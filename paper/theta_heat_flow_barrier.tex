\section{The genuine theta heat flow and a limit of curvature arguments}
\label{sec:thetaheat}
The de Bruijn--Newman deformation supplies an unusually
sharp test of which properties of the Jacobi kernel
can actually determine the zero locations.
We use the standard normalization of
Rodgers and Tao~\cite{RodgersTao,NewmanWu},
\begin{equation}
H_t(z)=\int_0^\infty e^{tu^2}\Phi(u)\cos(zu)\,du,
\qquad t\in\mathbb R,
\label{eq:heatH}
\end{equation}
where $\Phi$ is exactly the kernel used in this paper.
Our earlier full-line transform is $F(z)=2H_0(z)$.
The deformation satisfies the backward heat equation
\begin{equation}
\partial_tH_t(z)=-\partial_z^2H_t(z)
\label{eq:heatbackwards}
\end{equation}
by differentiation under the rapidly decreasing
Fourier integral.

\begin{theorem}[Curvature invariance cannot determine real zeros]
\label{thm:heatcurvatureobstruction}
Put $\Phi_t(u)=e^{tu^2}\Phi(u)$ and
$V_t(u)=-\log\Phi_t(u)$. For every $t\in\mathbb R$,
\begin{equation}
V_t'''(u)=V_0'''(u),\qquad u\in\mathbb R.
\label{eq:heatcurvatureinvariance}
\end{equation}
Nevertheless $H_{1/2}$ has only real zeros,
whereas $H_{-1}$ has nonreal zeros.
Thus even within the \emph{actual theta heat family},
identical third-order potential curvature
does not determine the Fourier real-zero property.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The identity $V_t(u)=V_0(u)-tu^2$
immediately gives \eqref{eq:heatcurvatureinvariance}.
By the de Bruijn--Newman theorem there is a finite
constant $\Lambda$ such that $H_t$ has only real
zeros exactly for $t\ge\Lambda$.
De Bruijn's upper bound gives $\Lambda\le1/2$.
Rodgers and Tao proved $\Lambda\ge0$
\cite{RodgersTao}. Hence $1/2\ge\Lambda$
and $-1<\Lambda$, proving the contrasting
zero-location statements.
\end{proof}

In particular the strict inequality
$V_0'''(u)>0$ proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:globalcurvature} is unchanged
by every heat deformation. It therefore holds
both at a time with all real zeros and
at a time with nonreal zeros.
This is an obstruction within the genuine theta
model, stronger in relevance than the
Gaussian examples of Section~\ref{sec:curvatureobstruction}.
It does not invalidate the bounded noncentral
Laguerre positivity band, and it does not decide
the case $t=0$.

\begin{proposition}[Coupled Laguerre heat hierarchy]
\label{prop:laguerreheat}
Define for real $x,y,t$
\[
Q_t(x,y)=H_t(x+iy)H_t(x-iy)
=\sum_{n\ge0}L_{n,t}(x)y^{2n}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}
\partial_t Q_t
=\tfrac12(\partial_y^2Q_t-\partial_x^2Q_t)
\label{eq:laguerreheatQ}
\end{equation}
and, for every integer $n\ge0$,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\partial_t L_{n,t}
=(n+1)(2n+1)L_{n+1,t}
-\tfrac12\partial_x^2L_{n,t}.}
\label{eq:laguerreheatcoupling}
\end{equation}
For the first nontrivial order,
\[
\partial_tL_{1,t}
=6L_{2,t}-\tfrac12\partial_x^2L_{1,t}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write $A=H_t(x+iy)$ and $B=H_t(x-iy)$.
Holomorphic differentiation gives
\begin{align*}
Q_{xx}&=A''B+2A'B'+AB'',\\
Q_{yy}&=-A''B+2A'B'-AB'',\\
Q_t&=-A''B-AB''.
\end{align*}
Subtracting yields \eqref{eq:laguerreheatQ}.
The coefficient of $y^{2n}$ in
$\tfrac12Q_{yy}$ is
$(n+1)(2n+1)L_{n+1,t}$;
equating coefficients proves
\eqref{eq:laguerreheatcoupling}.
The Fourier integrals and their derivatives
decay sufficiently rapidly to justify the
analytic differentiation and local
power-series expansion.
\end{proof}

The hierarchy exposes a concrete obstruction to
backward propagation of positivity.
Set $s=\frac12-t$ and write
$U_n(s,x)=L_{n,1/2-s}(x)$.
Then
\begin{equation}
\partial_sU_n
=\frac12\partial_x^2U_n
-(n+1)(2n+1)U_{n+1}.
\label{eq:backwardlaguerre}
\end{equation}
The spatial heat term diffuses, but the next
Laguerre order enters with a negative source.
Consequently positive Laguerre expressions at
$t=1/2$ cannot simply be propagated backwards
to $t=0$ by a scalar maximum principle.

\begin{proposition}[A sufficient, unproved heat comparison]
\label{prop:heatsufficient}
Suppose that for every $n\ge1$ there exists
a bounded nonnegative function $c_n(s)$ on
$[0,1/2]$ such that
\begin{equation}
(n+1)(2n+1)U_{n+1}(s,x)
\le c_n(s)U_n(s,x)
\label{eq:comparisonmissing}
\end{equation}
for every $s\in[0,1/2]$ and every real $x$.
Then all the generalized Laguerre inequalities
$L_{n,0}(x)\ge0$ hold for $n\ge1$ and real $x$.
In the Riemann entire-function setting,
the classical Laguerre--P\'olya criterion would
then imply RH.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $n\ge1$, equations
\eqref{eq:backwardlaguerre} and
\eqref{eq:comparisonmissing} give
\[
\partial_sU_n\ge
\tfrac12\partial_x^2U_n-c_n(s)U_n.
\]
At $s=0$ the transform $H_{1/2}$ has only
real zeros, so the classical generalized
Laguerre inequalities give $U_n(0,x)\ge0$.
The parabolic comparison principle, using
the rapid real-axis decay of these
Fourier transforms and their derivatives,
yields
\[
U_n(s,x)\ge
e^{-\int_0^s c_n(r)\,dr}
(P_sU_n(0,\cdot))(x)\ge0,
\]
where $P_s$ is the Gaussian heat semigroup.
Taking $s=1/2$ gives the conclusion.
\end{proof}

Condition \eqref{eq:comparisonmissing} is
\emph{not established}, and its truth for
the Riemann heat flow has not been verified.
It is a sufficient condition rather than an
equivalent reformulation; it may be
stronger than RH itself.
Any use in an RH proof would require an
honest uniform-in-$x$, all-order bound
for \eqref{eq:comparisonmissing}, or a
different mechanism controlling the
negative source in
\eqref{eq:backwardlaguerre}.

\paragraph{Reproducibility and prior work.}
The algebra behind
\eqref{eq:laguerreheatQ} and
the first-order coupling is checked in
\path{XiKernel/HeatFlowLaguerre.lean}.
The script
\path{certificates/xi_heat_flow_hierarchy_tests.py}
checks the identities symbolically, tests
the Gaussian coefficient hierarchy with
exact rational arithmetic for 100 orders,
and independently evaluates
\eqref{eq:heatcurvatureinvariance}
at four arguments and three heat times
to 65-digit precision.
The existence and properties of $\Lambda$,
the backward heat equation, and the
real-zero threshold are established
prior work \cite{RodgersTao,NewmanWu,Polymath15}.
The Lean code formalizes only algebraic
jet identities: it does not reprove
Rodgers--Tao or certify the genuine
heat-flow/Fourier analysis end to end.
The comparison condition remains open;
no proof of RH has been obtained.
