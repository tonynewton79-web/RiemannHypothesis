\section{A finite-certificate transfer for the spatial heat comparison}
\label{sec:compacttransfer}
The spatial comparison of
Theorem~\ref{thm:spatialmax} requires control of
$k_nU_{n+1}-c_nU_n$, where
$k_n=(n+1)(2n+1)$.
The global quotient $U_{n+1}/U_n$ is unsuitable for
certification near zeros, and fixed finite theta
truncations produce artificial negative odd-order
Laguerre expressions at large frequencies.
There is nevertheless a rigorous way to use
finite theta calculations on a \emph{compact}
heat--frequency rectangle without assuming
the desired positivity.

For $M\ge1$ let $H_{t,M}$ be the $M$-mode
theta transform from
Section~\ref{sec:heattail}, and put
$U_{n,M}(s,x)=L_n[H_{\frac12-s,M}](x)$.
Define, for $q_M=\pi(M+1)^2$,
\[
e_{n,M}=
\frac{3(2n+1)C_*}{2\cdot4^{2n}}\,
q_Me^{-q_M+1/16},
\qquad k_n=(n+1)(2n+1),
\]
where the finite positive constant $C_*$ is
defined in Corollary~\ref{cor:heatsources}.
Since $F_t=2H_t$ and generalized Laguerre
expressions are quadratic in their defining
function, the complex-strip estimate
\eqref{eq:heatlagerror} implies
\begin{equation}
|U_n(s,x)-U_{n,M}(s,x)|\le e_{n,M}
\quad(0\le s\le\tfrac12,\ x\in\mathbb R).
\label{eq:compacttransfererror}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Noncircular compact comparison transfer]
\label{thm:compacttransfer}
Fix an order $n\ge1$, a preterminal time
$0<S<1/2$, a radius $R>0$, and the rectangle
$K=[0,S]\times[-R,R]$.
Let $c:K\to[0,\infty)$ be a bounded function,
$0\le c\le C_K$. If the \emph{finite} theta
transform satisfies the rigorously certified
inequality
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
k_nU_{n+1,M}(s,x)-c(s,x)U_{n,M}(s,x)
\le -\Bigl(k_ne_{n+1,M}+C_Ke_{n,M}\Bigr)
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:compactcertcriterion}
\end{equation}
throughout $K$, then the \emph{genuine}
theta heat flow satisfies
\[
k_nU_{n+1}(s,x)\le c(s,x)U_n(s,x)
\quad((s,x)\in K).
\]
Neither the hypothesis nor the proof assumes
$U_n>0$, divides by a Laguerre expression, or
assumes RH.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $U=U_n$, $V=k_nU_{n+1}$, and put
subscript $M$ on their truncated analogues.
From \eqref{eq:compacttransfererror},
\[
V-cU
\le V_M-cU_M+
k_ne_{n+1,M}+c\,e_{n,M}
\le V_M-cU_M+
k_ne_{n+1,M}+C_Ke_{n,M}.
\]
The last expression is nonpositive
by \eqref{eq:compactcertcriterion}.
All inequalities hold pointwise, including
points at which $U_n$ vanishes.
\end{proof}

The estimate is effectively numerical. For
example, a coarse universal bound is
$C_*<100$. Indeed the first mode has
$q_1<4$ and $q_1-1/16>2$, yielding
\[
q_1^2e^{-(q_1-1/16)}
\left(\frac1{a_1}+\frac3{a_1^2}
+\frac6{a_1^3}+\frac6{a_1^4}\right)<38.
\]
For $m\ge2$, the rational incomplete-gamma
bound in \eqref{eq:heatrationalgate} and
a geometric ratio below $1/2$ imply a total
less than $3q_2e^{-(q_2-1/16)}<48$,
since $q_2<16$. Thus $C_*<86<100$.
Machin bounds $3<\pi<22/7$ and finite
positive Taylor sums for the exponential
permit completely rational upper enclosures
for each $e_{n,M}$.

\begin{proposition}[Why the compact restriction cannot
be omitted]\label{prop:oddcutoffcomparison}
For every finite $M$, fixed heat time
$t\in[0,1/2]$ and odd order $n\ge1$,
there exist arbitrarily large real frequencies
$x$ for which
\[
U_{n,M}(s,x)<0,\qquad
U_{n+1,M}(s,x)>0 .
\]
At those frequencies no nonnegative
coefficient $c(s,x)$ can satisfy even the
finite-mode inequality
$k_nU_{n+1,M}\le cU_{n,M}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The exact finite-cutoff asymptotic from
Theorem~\ref{thm:modepairsign} gives
\[
U_{j,M}(s,x)=
(-1)^j(j+1)D_M^2x^{-2j-4}
+O_{M,j,t}(|x|^{-2j-6}),
\quad D_M>0.
\]
For odd $j=n$, the leading coefficient
is negative; for even $j=n+1$ it is
positive. The asserted strict signs
therefore hold at sufficiently large
$|x|$. If $c\ge0$, then
$cU_{n,M}\le0<k_nU_{n+1,M}$.
\end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:compacttransfer} provides a
precise, reproducible target for a rigorous
rectangle-by-rectangle computer-assisted
attack. Its finite-mode hypothesis
\eqref{eq:compactcertcriterion} has \emph{not}
been established for the genuine theta flow
on the exhaustion of all compact rectangles.
A finite-mode absolute-error bound alone does
not imply the needed \emph{negative margin},
and the asymptotic obstruction above rules
out imposing it globally with fixed $M$.

The exact triangle-inequality gates are
kernel-checked in
\path{XiKernel/ThetaCompactSpatialTransfer.lean}.
The independent standard-library rational
checker
\path{certificates/xi_compact_spatial_transfer_exact.py}
tests the error coefficients and the
transfer inequality. Neither test
establishes the missing finite-mode
sign inequality throughout any
exhaustion of the complete heat--frequency
domain. Therefore this is a
necessary refinement of the proof method,
not a proof of RH.
