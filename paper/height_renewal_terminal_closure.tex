\subsubsection{Terminal closure and a scaled even heat-flow control}
\label{subsec:terminal-closure-renewal}

Local Rouch\'e margins protect individual theta-flow
zero pairs. The terminal limit at $t=0$ and the
all-height continuation at positive heat times
are logically different questions.

\begin{theorem}[Terminal real-zero closure]
\label{thm:terminal-hurwitz}
Suppose $t_j>0$ tends to zero and, for every $j$,
all zeros of the genuine theta transform $H_{t_j}$
are real. Then $H_0$ has only real zeros.
Consequently the Riemann hypothesis holds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each compact set of complex frequencies,
$\Phi(u)e^{u^2/2+Y u}$ is an integrable
majorant of the heat Fourier integrands.
Dominated convergence gives
$H_{t_j}\to H_0$ locally uniformly.
If $H_0$ had a nonreal zero $z_0$,
a sufficiently small disk about $z_0$ could be
chosen disjoint from the real axis, with no
boundary zeros. The boundary minimum of
$|H_0|$ is positive. Rouch\'e's theorem
then gives a zero of $H_{t_j}$ in the same
nonreal disk for all sufficiently large $j$,
a contradiction.
\end{proof}

The premise concerns \emph{all} zeros at each
selected positive time. Individual finite-height
certificates cannot supply it without an
additional global tail argument.
Once the premise is established, no separate
terminal escape-from-infinity argument is needed.

\begin{theorem}[Even high-height heat collisions on arbitrarily
short intervals]\label{thm:even-high-collision}
For $A\ge\varepsilon>0$, set
\[
S=(A+\varepsilon)^2+(A+2\varepsilon)^2,\qquad
P=(A+\varepsilon)^2(A+2\varepsilon)^2,
\]
and
\begin{equation}
Q_s(z)=z^4+(12s-S)z^2+P-2Ss+12s^2.
\label{eq:height-renewal-quartic}
\end{equation}
The polynomial is even and satisfies the exact
backward-heat equation
$\partial_sQ_s=\partial_z^2Q_s$.
It has simple real zeros at
$\pm(A+\varepsilon),\pm(A+2\varepsilon)$
when $s=0$. At some
$s_*\in(0,\varepsilon^2)$ its positive
and negative zero pairs collide at positions
of modulus at least $A/\sqrt2$.
At $s=\varepsilon^2$ it has no real zero.
Hence, for any $R>0$ and $\delta>0$,
there are real-rooted even backward-heat data
which lose real-rootedness beyond height $R$
before time $\delta$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Direct differentiation verifies the PDE.
Writing $v=z^2$, the quadratic discriminant is
\begin{align}
\Delta(s)&=(2A\varepsilon+3\varepsilon^2)^2
-16Ss+96s^2,\label{eq:height-renewal-discr}\\
\Delta(\varepsilon^2)&=\varepsilon^2
(-28A^2-84A\varepsilon+25\varepsilon^2)<0.
\end{align}
The second sign holds for $A\ge\varepsilon>0$,
while $\Delta(0)>0$.
Continuity gives $s_*\in(0,\varepsilon^2)$
with $\Delta(s_*)=0$. Its repeated $v$-root is
\[
v_*=\frac{S-12s_*}{2}>
\frac{S-12\varepsilon^2}{2}\ge\frac{A^2}{2}>0,
\]
because
$S-12\varepsilon^2-A^2
=(A-\varepsilon)(A+7\varepsilon)\ge0$.
Thus the collisions occur at
$\pm\sqrt{v_*}$ on the real axis.
At time $\varepsilon^2$ the completed square
\[
Q_{\varepsilon^2}(z)=
\left(z^2+\frac{12\varepsilon^2-S}{2}\right)^2
-\frac{\Delta(\varepsilon^2)}4>0
\quad(z\in\mathbb R)
\]
shows that there are no remaining real zeros.
Choose $A>\sqrt2R$ and
$0<\varepsilon<\min(A,\sqrt\delta)$.
\end{proof}

The gap between the initial adjacent positive
zeros is $\varepsilon$ and it closes at $s_*$.
Its signed shielding identity therefore reaches
$4\int_0^{s_*}\mathcal D(s)\,ds=\varepsilon^2$.
This confirms at arbitrary scales the earlier
warning: a strict integrated shielding budget
cannot be derived merely from its own endpoint
gap identity. This polynomial does not represent
the theta function; it is an adversarial test
of generic heat-flow continuation claims.

\begin{proposition}[A countable complete-proof target]
\label{prop:sequence-heattimes}
RH is equivalent to the existence of
a sequence of positive times $t_j\downarrow0$
at which every zero of the genuine $H_{t_j}$
is real.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
One implication is Theorem~\ref{thm:terminal-hurwitz}.
Conversely RH is equivalent to $\Lambda\le0$,
and the classical de Bruijn--Newman theorem
then supplies real zeros for every $t>0$.
\end{proof}

The remaining RH-level obligation is a
theta-specific \emph{all-height} zero-exclusion
argument at positive heat times arbitrarily
close to zero. Time-renewed contour margins
on a finite collection of disks do not
meet this quantifier, and the heat PDE plus
evenness cannot supply a uniform renewal time.
The sequence criterion is a reduction, not
an independent proof of RH.
